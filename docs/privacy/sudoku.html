Sudoku — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Sudoku app.

Data we collect

None. Sudoku does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, and high scores are saved locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or use the in-app Reset Data option in Settings.

Advertising and purchases

This version of Sudoku does not display ads or offer in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Sudoku does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds advertising, in-app purchases, or any data collection, this page will be updated first.

Contact
