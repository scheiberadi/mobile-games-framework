# Map rework (pannable landscape with places, walking characters, settings)

Date: 2026-09-25. Status: design approved by the user section by section in conversation; written spec awaiting the user's review. Part of Eva's Learning World (`EvasLearningWorld/`). This is step 1 of two: step 2 (side-view walking art for the child and Eva) is a separate spec, plan and phone test, and is not designed here.

## Goal

The current Map is three building icons in a row on a soft landscape, with Eva and the child's character standing large on the right. Replace it with a painted landscape that behaves like a small world: the house in the middle, other places spread around it with roads leading from the house to each, small characters playing near the house at first and later near the last place visited, and a walk along the road to a tapped place before it opens. The world must have room for the roughly eleven places the product plans (House, School, Store, Playground now; Arcade, Zoo and farm, Science lab, Workshop, Art studio, Brain gym, Friends' park later), which a single 1440 x 900 screen cannot hold at the 240-unit tap minimum.

## Decisions (from the design conversation)

- Layout: house in the centre, School and Store around it (a triangle, School upper-left clear of the top-left corner, Store lower-right), roads radiating from the house. Not an island; an ordinary landscape.
- World size: a painted landscape about 2880 x 1350 units (twice the screen wide, 1.5 times tall). The screen is a 1440 x 900 window on it.
- Places are added by new art plus one catalogue entry. Only places that exist are shown: no locks, no "coming soon".
- Characters are small (about 160 units tall). They walk to a tapped place along the roads and the place opens on arrival.
- Step 1 uses today's front-facing character rigs, slid along the road in small hops. Real side-view walking art is step 2.
- Art is generated by the user with ChatGPT from prompts Claude writes (house workflow). Claude draws only the gear icon.

## Data (Rules layer, pure C#)

`Places` (new, `Rules/Places.cs`): `enum PlaceId { House, School, Store }` and `sealed class Place` with `Id`, `ScreenKey` (name of the app-layer `ScreenId`, a string so Rules stays engine-free), `Position` (world units, centre origin, house at 0,0), `Road` (list of waypoints from the house to the place, world units), `StandingSpot` (where the characters stand beside the place), `BuildingSprite`, `RoadSprite`, `VoiceKey`. `Places.All` returns the declared, fixed order. Content today: House at (0,0), School and Store around it, each with the `screen key` of the existing screens. Playground is added when its games exist.

`MapPath` (new, pure): `Route(from, to)` returns the waypoint list between two places, always through the house junction (a trip School to Store walks School road backwards to the house, then the Store road). `Duration(route)` returns the trip time: constant speed, capped at 2.5 seconds (long trips walk faster). `PositionAt(route, t)` returns the point along the route for a normalised time, used by the screen.

`MapCamera` (new, pure): clamps a requested view centre so the 1440 x 900 window stays inside the world, and `CentreOn(point)` returns the clamped centre.

## Screen (App layer)

`MapScreen` is rebuilt (same `ScreenId.Map`):

- Layers from back to front: landscape backdrop (one picture; if it exceeds the texture size limit it is cut into two halves), one road picture per place, one building picture per place (tap targets, at least 240 units), then the two characters.
- The world sits under a content transform; dragging moves it (clamped by `MapCamera`). A drag that starts on a building is still a tap if the pointer barely moved.
- On show: the view centres on the characters' standing spot (see below).
- Tap on a building: `Voice.Say(place.VoiceKey)`, lock dragging and further taps, walk both characters along `MapPath.Route`, the view follows them, and on arrival `Navigator.Show(place screen)`. If the route is empty (already at that place) the screen opens at once. The voice line is never awaited.
- Characters: first launch they stand beside the house; afterwards beside the last place visited (`Progress.LastPlace`, saved on arrival). They keep the existing tap-to-wave and idle behaviour while standing. While walking they hop along the route (Eva bounces; the child bobs gently).
- The global Home button stays hidden on the Map (existing behaviour); other screens' Home returns to the Map, so the child sees the characters where they left them.
- Top-left gear button (small icon, tap area still 240 units): opens the parent gate.

## Tutorial

The tutorial hand keeps pointing at the right building (`GoToSchool`: School; `GoToStore`: Store; House stays as today). School and Store are placed so both lie inside the first view around the house, so the hand never needs scrolling. Hand positions are computed from world positions through the content transform.

## Settings

- `ParentGateScreen` (new): one adult-knowledge question with multiple-choice answers, randomised from a pool of about 20 culturally neutral questions (per the parent spec). A correct answer opens the settings panel; a wrong answer or leaving returns to the Map. It is an accidental-access barrier, not security.
- `SettingsScreen` (new): a few big-icon controls, no reading beyond digits: music on/off, voice volume, and "start over" with a second confirmation. Which controls the current audio code supports is checked in the plan; any control that needs new machinery is dropped from this step rather than built.
- Both screens are added to the all-screens audits (tap targets, no text except digits).

## Art (produced by the user with ChatGPT; Claude writes the prompts in the plan)

- Roadless landscape backdrop, about 2880 x 1350, same style as the house art, clear ground left where future places will appear.
- One building picture each for House, School and Store, on a clean removable background.
- One road picture per place, on a clean removable background, aligned to the waypoints in `Places`.
- Placeholders are used to build and test; the phone check waits for the real art.

## Testing

Rules (edit mode, engine-free): `Places` (non-empty, ids unique, fixed order, every place has screen, sprites, road with at least two waypoints); `MapPath` (route between every pair goes through the house, duration capped at 2.5 seconds and shorter for short trips, `PositionAt` endpoints); `MapCamera` (clamping at every edge, centring near an edge).

App (edit mode): buildings at least 240 and non-overlapping; tap starts the walk and opens the screen only after arrival (advance the walk in the test); a second tap during the walk is ignored; the last place is saved and restored and the characters stand there on show; the tutorial hand targets computed from world positions; parent gate accepts only the right answer and returns to the Map otherwise; settings controls change their saved values; the audits include the new screens. The existing suite keeps passing (Map tests updated for the rebuilt screen).

Phone review is the real gate: does the world feel like a place, do the roads and buildings line up, is dragging comfortable, is the walk short enough, does the view follow smoothly, is the gear discreet.

## Out of scope

Side-view walking art (step 2), the Playground games, the other planned places, locks or progress on places, changing other screens, a free-scrolling world beyond the fixed landscape, a full settings system beyond the three controls.
